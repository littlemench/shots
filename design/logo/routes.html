Shots · Brand exploration · v0.1

Logo routes

Five directions built around the idea of a photograph as a “shot”. Each route shows a mark, a wordmark and a lockup (the two together), then tests it on a photo in the gallery header and at favicon sizes. Everything is monochrome on purpose: the photos bring the colour.

Recommendation

Lead with A (Viewfinder)

A is the only route where the mark does something in the product. The corner brackets can become the UI's own language: the outline on the selected thumbnail, the focus ring, a loading state, the frame around the credit line. The mark literally frames content, which is "photos first" as a symbol. It's built on the same 2px pixel grid as pixelarticons, so logo and icons feel like one system, and it stays crisp at 16px.

B is the boldest option, but a fully pixel wordmark is a big personality to put beside the photography. Pairing A's pixel mark with a clean sans wordmark keeps the pixel detail in one place: the mark and the icons. E is the safe, editorial alternative if the pixel direction starts to feel too playful.

  1. Pick one or two routes to take further.
  2. Refine: letterspacing and weight, a custom-drawn wordmark, and how the mark and wordmark align.
  3. Apply the favourite in the gallery prototype, plus the favicon and the social preview image.
