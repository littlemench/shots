Shots · Brand exploration · v0.2

A wordmark with a secret

No separate logo. Just the name, set quietly, with one small photographic reference built into the letters. Most people will only read "Shots", and the few who look closer find the detail. Hover over each one to see its easter egg come alive. The detail can double as the favicon, so there's no need for a separate mark.

First take

Focus point, with Full stop as the serif alternative

01 Focus is the most "hidden in plain sight": a single square in the O, the exact mark a camera shows when it has locked focus. It's the same shape as a pixel from the icon set, it works in any case or weight, and the O on its own makes a strong favicon. On the site, that square could blink amber for a moment when a photo opens: a small, private joke.

06 Full stop is the editorial version of the same thought: the full stop is a single pixel, the smallest possible shot. 02 Stopped down is the cleverest, but also the most likely to read as a logo rather than a letter.
